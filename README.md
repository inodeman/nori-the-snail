## Story

Nori is the smallest snail in a garden that feels like the whole world. When the storm comes, Papa asks him to stay on his shell. Then Nori sees someone even smaller — a baby worm on a drowning leaf.

Integrity is quiet. It just turns you around.
Loyalty is Papa going back into the rain.
Family is who waits in the morning.
